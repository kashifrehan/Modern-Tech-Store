GOOD TO KNOW

Are the products shown here available right now?

This website introduces the types of products and services we can help with. Availability changes, so ask us to confirm the exact model before making a payment.

Will you help me choose within my budget?

Yes. Tell us what you need the device for and your budget. We’ll help you compare practical options and explain the trade-offs.

What about product condition and warranty?

These details can differ by item. Before you pay, confirm whether the product is new, used, refurbished or open-box, and ask who provides the warranty and what it covers.

No. The quotation calculator currently uses illustrative demo prices and delivery fees. They are not live prices, stock information or a confirmed offer.

Can you help with a PC build or upgrade?

PC building, upgrades, setup and basic technical support are part of the services Modern Tech Store aims to provide. Share your requirements so we can discuss what is possible.
